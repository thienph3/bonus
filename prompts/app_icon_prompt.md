Icon concept:
- Two overlapping arrows or document shapes representing "matching" / "pairing" of debts
- Incorporate a checkmark or balance symbol to represent successful reconciliation
- Use a color palette of deep blue (#1565C0) and green (#2E7D32) to convey trust and financial accuracy
- Flat/material design style suitable for Windows desktop taskbar (clear at 16x16 and 256x256)
- No text in the icon
- Square format with slight rounded corners
- Professional, minimal, suitable for an internal enterprise accounting tool

Output: 256x256 PNG with transparent background.
